Root cause: the RunPod endpoint was configured with workersMax: 1 — only one image could generate at a time, full stop. A 2nd and 3rd shopper weren't generating in parallel; they were standing in a single-file line behind whoever clicked first. This was a RunPod account setting, not something fixable in the widget's code.

Measured in the actual widget

Real test: 3 browser tabs, staggered starts (2nd tab +1s, 3rd tab +9s after that), full round trip — image upload → Shopify App Proxy → Remix proxy → PHP backend → RunPod → back. Total span: 64s, matching a stopwatch read of 1.08 min almost exactly.

!

The extra ~12-19s per shopper on top of pure RunPod generation is image upload, the Shopify App Proxy hop, and the Remix→PHP round trip — real cost a shopper actually pays, invisible to the API-only test above. The pattern is identical either way (each shopper waits behind whoever clicked first) — this just confirms it holds end-to-end, not only at the RunPod layer.

Fix applied: workersMax 1 → 10

RunPod doesn't offer literal "unlimited" workers — this account's hard quota across all endpoints is 10, which is now the endpoint's ceiling.

Shopper Before (max 1) First burst after fix (max 10)
1st27s28s
2nd44s51s
3rd64s60s
!

The first burst right after raising the limit didn't look faster yet — and that's expected. RunPod scales workers reactively: it only provisions the 2nd and 3rd GPU worker once it sees sustained queue delay, and spinning up a fresh worker (loading the model onto a new GPU) itself takes real time. Shoppers 2 and 3 landing close together (51s, 60s) rather than strictly one-behind-the-other (the old 44s→64s pattern) is early evidence the extra workers came online during this burst. Once those workers are warm, a repeat burst should land noticeably closer to the ~15-30s single-shopper range for all three.

What to expect at higher concurrency

With workersMax now 10: up to 10 shoppers can generate in true parallel once workers are warm. An 11th+ simultaneous shopper still queues behind the first 10.
